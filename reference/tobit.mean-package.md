# tobit.mean: Calculate Descriptive Statistics of Censored Vectors

Calculate descriptive statistics of left- and right-censored numeric
vectors: the arithmetic mean, geometric mean, standard deviation, and
percent coefficient of variation. Censoring is accounted for by maximum
likelihood estimation with the Tobit model (Tobin (1958)
[doi:10.2307/1907382](https://doi.org/10.2307/1907382) ), using the
convex reparameterization of the likelihood described by Olsen (1978)
[doi:10.2307/1911445](https://doi.org/10.2307/1911445) , rather than by
ignoring or substituting censored values.
